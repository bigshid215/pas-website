Bigger and Better Business

Phi Alpha Sigma Chapter.

Bigger and Better Business Bigger and Better Business became the first national program of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc., following its introduction at the Tenth Anniversary Conclave in Philadelphia in December 1924. The program was established to promote Black-owned businesses by strengthening business knowledge, improving efficiency, and increasing competitiveness within the communities they serve. Phi Alpha Sigma Chapter Key Initiatives • Black Spend Initiative: Supports Black-owned businesses by increasing awareness, encouraging intentional spending, and strengthening the circulation of Black dollars within our communities. • Financial Literacy and Mentorship: Advances financial literacy, promotes investment, and supports generational wealth in the Black community. • Adopt-A-Business Initiative: Provides direct support to small Black-owned businesses through resources, guidance, and business development assistance. Phi Alpha Sigma Chapter Bigger and Better Business Initiative As the Director of Bigger and Better Business, the focus is on advancing economic growth, strengthening collaboration, and creating meaningful opportunities within the community. The vision behind these efforts is shaped by F.O.C.U.S. 1. Finance – Building financial literacy and wealth. 2. Outreach – Expanding awareness and support. 3. Collaboration – Creating strong partnerships. 4. Unity – Strengthening collective impact. 5. Strategy – Driving intentional growth.
