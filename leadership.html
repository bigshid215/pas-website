President

Bro. Dr. Rahkon Ross

President Message

Dr. Rahkon “Jersey” Ross is a leader defined by resilience, service, and an unwavering pursuit of excellence. A Charter Member and current Chapter President of the Phi Alpha Sigma Alumni Chapter of Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity, Inc., he was initiated in Spring 2009 through the Kappa Upsilon Sigma Chapter in South Jersey. Since then, the principles of Brotherhood, Scholarship, and Service have guided every step of his journey. Raised in Asbury Park, New Jersey, Dr. Ross learned early that education could transform not only his own life, but also inspire those coming behind him. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Jersey City University in 2009, followed by a Master’s degree in Hospitality and Tourism Management in 2011. While building his career with the Department of War–Army, first at Fort Monmouth and later at Aberdeen Proving Ground, Dr. Ross continued to pursue academic excellence. He earned an MBA in 2013 and a Master’s degree in Program Management in 2014 from Strayer University, graduating summa cum laude and receiving the institution’s highest Community Service Award. He later earned a fourth Master’s degree in Acquisition and Contract Management from the Florida Institute of Technology in 2019. His professional path has included service as an Integrated Logistics Support Manager, Contract Specialist, Contracting Officer, and Senior Program Analyst overseeing two of the nation’s most critical defense programs: the Virginia-class and Ohio-class submarine programs. In 2023, Dr. Ross earned a Ph.D. in Cybersecurity from Northcentral University in just 26 months, graduating summa cum laude. His pursuit continues, with plans to begin his fifth Master’s degree in Systems Engineering at the Naval Postgraduate School in 2027 and ultimately pursue a second Ph.D. in Engineering. Dr. Ross’s legacy is bigger than degrees, titles, or awards. It is a powerful reminder that adversity does not define destiny and that discipline, faith, and determination can turn purpose into impact. “Always remember, you have within you the strength, the patience, and the passion to reach for the stars to change the world.” ~Harriet Tubman
